Privacy Policy

Apex Lifts is a mobile app where lifters record a lift at their gym, have it checked by other members, and compete for titles on that gym's board. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Short version
We collect what the app needs to run: your account, your lifts and their videos, your location at the moment you submit a lift, and a token so we can send you notifications. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. You can delete your account and everything attached to it from inside the app.

3.What other members can see

  • Your handle, display name, weight class, home gym, and approved lifts are public inside the app.
  • Videos of approved lifts can be viewed by any signed-in member. Videos of pending lifts are visible only to you and to verifiers reviewing them. Videos of rejected lifts are visible only to you and to verifiers.
  • Your email address, exact location coordinates, and birth year are never shown to other members. Only your age class is.

4.Who else handles your data

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and are not allowed to use it for their own purposes.

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage, and sign-in system. Data is stored on servers in the United States.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and delivers push notifications. Apple's own privacy policy governs what Apple sees.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect the safety of our users, or as part of a sale or merger of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to it.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

5.How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile, and lifts stay until you delete them.
  • Videos stay for as long as the lift they belong to exists. You can withdraw a pending lift at any time, which deletes its video.
  • Verification records and credits are kept for as long as needed to pay verifiers and investigate fraud.
  • Backups are kept for a short period and then overwritten.

6.Deleting your account

Open the app, go to your profile, and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, lifts, videos, verifier records, reports, and sign-in details. Titles you held become open for other lifters. Deletion cannot be undone. You can also email us at maraafhservices@gmail.com and we will delete the account for you.

7.Your choices

  • Location. You can deny location access in iOS Settings. You will still be able to browse boards but will not be able to submit lifts, since location is how we confirm you are at the gym.
  • Notifications. Turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Profile data. Edit your handle, display name, home gym, and bodyweight in the app at any time.

8.Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. If you live in California, you also have the right to know what categories of data we collect and to be free from discrimination for exercising your rights. We do not sell or share personal data as those terms are defined in California law. To make a request, email maraafhservices@gmail.com. We will verify your identity and respond within the time the law requires.

9.Age

Apex Lifts is for people 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10.Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is restricted by per-user rules so members can only read what this policy says they can read. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password or Sign in with Apple.

11.Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.

3.How lifts and titles work

  • You submit a lift by recording a video at the gym and entering the weight. We record your location at that moment to confirm you are at the gym you claim.
  • Other members acting as verifiers watch the video and decide whether it is a completed lift at the stated weight. A lift is approved or rejected when enough verifiers agree. Ties are decided by an additional verifier.
  • Titles such as Bench King are held by the lifter with the heaviest approved lift of that kind at that gym. A title changes hands as soon as a heavier lift is approved.
  • Verifier decisions are final within the app. We may re-review any lift and reverse a decision if we find an error or abuse. We may also remove a lift or title at any time if we believe it was obtained by breaking these terms.
  • Titles are a feature of the app. They have no cash value and do not represent any official record or ranking.

  • Approve lifts you have not watched, approve lifts as a favor, coordinate with other verifiers, or otherwise game verification or verifier pay.
