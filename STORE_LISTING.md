**Description**:
Apex Lifts turns your gym into a leaderboard.

Every gym in the United States is on the map, and every gym holds three titles: Bench King, Squat King and Deadlift King. The heaviest verified single at that gym wears the crown until someone beats it.

HOW IT WORKS
• Open the map and find your gym. Tens of thousands of gyms are already listed.
• Film your lift. One take, one rep, plates visible.
• Submit it at the gym. Your location confirms you are there.
• Two other members review the video and confirm the lift. Ties go to a third.
• Approved lifts go on the gym's board. Beat the current holder and the title is yours.

RANK BEYOND YOUR GYM
See where your verified lifts stand against lifters nearby, across your state, and across the country. Filter by weight class and age class.

VERIFY AND EARN
Members can become verifiers and earn a small credit for every decision that matches the final verdict.

NO FAKE NUMBERS
Every lift on a board has a video behind it and a location stamp. Boards start empty and fill with real lifts from real members.

Apex Lifts is for lifters 16 and older. Lifting heavy carries risk. Train with spotters and safeties and follow your gym's rules.
